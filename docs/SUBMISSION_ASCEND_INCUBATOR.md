## 7. What are you building, and who is it for?

Every Ascend project is sold the same climb: launch, Ascended status, HyperCore spot, then HIP-3 perps. The third rung is a one-shot, irreversible ceremony gated by five facts a builder fixes at launch without knowing they matter: supply, decimals, metadata mutability, mintability and transfer behaviour. LOCKAGE reads those back before launch, computes the HyperCore graduation envelope exactly as HyperCore will, seals the token's bridge identity, and refuses any holder crossing whose route has drifted. It is for builders launching on Ascend and for their holders. Token scanners score a token after the fact; LOCKAGE computes the HyperCore rules, seals identity, and enforces it on chain. We reproduced on Elysium testnet that after one rename the raw router still accepts the old mirror while LockGate refuses, and 625 of 3,249 (19%) surveyed testnet tokens can never graduate because of supply or decimals.

## 19. Are there important technical, security, or operational risks we should know about?

LockGate pulls a holder's tokens and forwards them to the bridge escrow wallet in the same transaction and holds nothing afterwards. The contracts have no admin keys, no upgradeability and no server key; every write is signed by the user's own wallet. They are unaudited and tested only on testnet: 74 unit, fuzz and fork tests plus a real-wallet browser suite. Known limits, published: the bytecode scan cannot prove a negative (it is scored against ground truth and its misses are named), the observed challenge window is not yet measured, and the deposit-wallet leg is designed but not executed because Kinetiq has not published the factory.
